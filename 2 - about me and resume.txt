THE XYZ BULLET FORMULA
"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."
Weak: "Responsible for data visualization."
Better: "Built an interactive dashboard used by 4 teams by redesigning data pipelines in Python and JavaScript."
If no defensible numbers exist: "Designed an interactive dashboard that helped cross-functional teams explore geospatial trends." Never invent numbers.
Website writing tips: use precise action verbs; start with outcomes; explain who benefited; short paragraphs and scannable bullets; expand beyond resume where you have evidence.
