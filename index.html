Vibefolio

Ride this line from an empty folder to your own published portfolio. Step 0 gets you ready. At Steps 1 to 4 you answer a few questions, copy one prompt into your AI agent, and check the result; your site goes live at Step 4. Step 5 is yours to keep exploring. Times are a guide: take as long as your own content needs.

Your answers stay in this browser only.
    0

    Setup

    Get your tools ready

    Your turn10–30 min

    You need a GitHub account, two free apps, one AI coding agent of your choice, and an empty folder. Tick each box as you go.

    1

    Your style

    Decide how it should feel

    Your turn10–20 min

    You don’t need to know design. Tell your agent what you love, and it will build a style from it that is yours: colors, fonts, shapes and layout.

    Nothing is final. The agent shows you a plan first, and you can change anything before it builds.

    2

    About you

    Say who you are

    Your turn10+ min

    Paste your whole resume. Your agent picks what belongs on a portfolio, writes it up, and shows you the text before it builds. You fix anything wrong in the plan or after.

    3

    Your projects

    Show your best work

    Your turn10+ min

    Drop your work into one folder. Your agent looks at every file, groups it into projects and drafts the text. You correct its draft before anything is built.

    4

    Review and publish

    Look, fix, go live

    Your turn30+ min

    Open your site in the browser and follow the review checklist at your own pace. Mark what needs a fix, ask your agent to help, then re-test before publishing.

    5

    Optional · after the workshop

    Keep it growing

    Optionalanytime

    Your site is live, and this is where the fun starts. Nothing here is required. Take it as a menu of ideas for the weeks after today: tell richer stories, add a bit of play, and keep adding new work.

    Your capability is now limited only by your imagination.
