Who Gets to Sit Here? A Geography of Public Benches

The 15-Minute City, According to My Grandmother

Fall 2025 · Interview + StoryMap

Compared conventional walking-access maps with 12 oral histories about errands, social networks, winter weather, and the meaning of “nearby.”

Invisible Borders: Where Campus Changes

Spring 2025 · Photo Essay + Participatory Mapping

About

I’m a Geography student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, concentrating in Human Geography and earning a Certificate in Digital Studies (expected May 2028). I study how people experience everyday places, from park benches and walking routes to the edges of campus, using interviews, participatory mapping and GIS.

Outside class I co-chair the Geography Club, volunteer with a community oral history project, and work as a bookseller and café assistant at The Wandering Bookmark. I like zines, public transit, indie bookstores, and collecting maps with questionable north arrows.
